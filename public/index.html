How it works

Scout

Finds what fits

Scout scans the Hub on a schedule and scores new models, datasets, Spaces, papers and repos against the themes you care about. Authors you follow always surface.

You

Approve the picks

Nothing downloads until you say so. Licenses that need your acceptance are flagged before anything lands on the drive.

Drive

Stashes it offline

Plug the drive into any of your machines and the run starts by itself. Scout writes only inside its stash folder and never deletes a version you already have.

What you get

One stash, many machines

Macs and Linux boxes (a Raspberry Pi included) share one drive. The dashboard shows which machine has it and what is left to download.

A bench for your models

Score local models on decision tasks, like picking the right tool or template, before you trust them in a workflow.

Safe to eject

Scout tells you when a run is done and the drive is safe to unplug, and resumes partial downloads next time.

Built on Cloudflare

The scout runs on Cloudflare Workers with D1 and KV. The heavy files never pass through the cloud, only through your own connection to the Hub.
